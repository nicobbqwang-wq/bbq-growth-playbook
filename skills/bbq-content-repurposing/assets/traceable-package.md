# Traceable content package / 可追溯内容包

Use the user's requested language. Include only cards needed for the requested channels and task.

## Claim cards / 主张卡

| ID | Claim and type | Source locator | Attribution and verification | Scope / limits | Use decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | Fact, advice, inference or hypothetical example | Source record + section/paragraph | Who claims this? Source-supported, independently checked, disputed or unknown? | Date, sample, context and what it does not establish | Use, qualify or exclude |

A supplied statement is not independently verified merely because it was pasted into the prompt. Suggested actions must remain suggestions. Use synthetic examples only with their fictional status visible in the publishable draft.

## Delivery / 交付

1. Requested draft(s), ready to review, with necessary attribution and limits in the text.
2. Separate editorial map: meaningful passage → claim ID(s) → source locator.
3. Unresolved items and link-check status. Do not claim publication.

For a source revision, list changed claim IDs, affected passages and the proposed correction. Retain the prior source reference rather than silently rewriting the history.

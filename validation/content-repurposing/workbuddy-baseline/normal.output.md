# LinkedIn Draft — For Independent Consultants

> **Reader question:** How do I turn a long article into a LinkedIn post that actually helps someone — instead of just a teaser with a link?

---

You wrote a 2,000-word article. Now you want a LinkedIn post from it.

The mistake most consultants make: they compress the whole thing into a paragraph and tack on a link. The reader learns nothing and scrolls past.

A better approach: pick **one** takeaway from the article — the single insight that matters most to the person reading your post — and explain it fully enough that they get the value without clicking anywhere.

Two things to carry over from the original:

- The **evidence** behind the claim — a data point, a step, an example.
- The **limits** of that evidence — what it does and doesn't prove.

A longer article can support several posts, each built around a different reader question. But each post should work on its own.

If your LinkedIn post can't help someone without a link, it isn't an adaptation — it's a headline.

## LinkedIn draft

Have a useful article but struggle to turn it into a LinkedIn post? Start with one question an independent consulting client might ask.

Choose one takeaway from your article that answers it. Keep the supporting evidence and any limits, then explain it in language that fits the reader’s situation.

Give readers something they can use directly in the post. A longer article can provide material for several posts, each answering a different question.

Try it with one article: write down one specific reader question, then adapt only the material needed to answer it.

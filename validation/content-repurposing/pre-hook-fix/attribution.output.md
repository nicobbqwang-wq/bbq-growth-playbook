## LinkedIn draft

Independent consultants: “easier to follow” is useful feedback. It doesn’t establish business impact.

Consider this fictional example: in Cedar Research’s May 2025 pilot, 12 volunteers tested a checklist. Seven said it was easier to follow.

There was no control group and no conversion or revenue tracking. The feedback supports a narrow observation about perceived ease of use; it cannot establish improved conversions or revenue.

This was Cedar Research’s fictional pilot, not my project.

My proposed next step is to test the checklist with consenting readers. I haven’t done that yet. I’d ask where they get stuck and which instructions need clarification.

Before testing your own client checklist, define the question you want to answer. Can readers follow the steps? Does it affect business outcomes? Each question needs its own evidence.

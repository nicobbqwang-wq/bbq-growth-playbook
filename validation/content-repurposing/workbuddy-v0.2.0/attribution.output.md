## LinkedIn Draft

A small pilot by Cedar Research tested whether a checklist was easier to follow. In May 2025, 12 volunteers tried it. Seven said yes.

That's a tiny, self-selected sample with no control group. No revenue was tracked, no conversions measured. The result tells us the checklist felt easier for a majority of twelve people — and nothing beyond that.

Still, a dozen people and one honest question — "was this easier to use?" — is a cheap first filter before you invest in a full study.

This was Cedar Research's pilot, not mine. My next step — not yet started — is to test the same idea with consenting readers and see whether the finding holds up with a different group.

If you work solo: run the small, honest check before you scale the claim. Know what your data says and what it doesn't.

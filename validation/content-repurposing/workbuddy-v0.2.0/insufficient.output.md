## Why no draft was produced

This skill adapts an **existing** article into a channel-specific draft. It does not
write a new article from a title alone. A label does not turn new writing into source
adaptation, and inventing a body to fit the title would violate the skill's core rule:
preserve the source's dates, sample limits, attribution, and uncertainty.

Writing a plausible-looking article from scratch — even one that *sounds* credible —
would mean fabricating claims, statistics, and attribution. That is exactly what the
"Make it credible" constraint forbids: credibility comes from real source material,
not confident prose.

So instead of a fabricated draft, below is a short input outline you can fill in or
paste over. Once you return it, I can produce the WeChat draft immediately.

## LinkedIn Draft

When you adapt a professional article for LinkedIn, choose one useful takeaway and build the post around it. A longer article can give you material for several posts—one takeaway each—so you don't need to compress everything into a single update.

A few things to keep in mind as you adapt:

- **Retain the evidence and its limits.** If the source's claim depends on a specific sample, context, or caveat, keep that qualifier in the post. The source calls for retaining evidence and limits—so don't drop the boundaries to make the idea sound stronger.

- **Adjust the explanation to your reader.** Think about what your reader needs to follow the takeaway. The source advises adjusting the explanation to the reader, so consider how to frame the idea so it lands for whoever is reading your post.

- **Make the post useful without a click.** The adapted post should help the reader without a click. Aim to give the reader something they can use from the post itself.

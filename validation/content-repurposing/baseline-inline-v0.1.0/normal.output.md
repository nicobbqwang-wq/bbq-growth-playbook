### LinkedIn draft

Have a useful article you’re struggling to turn into a LinkedIn post? Start with one question your reader needs answered.

Choose one takeaway from the article that answers it. Keep the supporting evidence and any limits, then explain what it means for your reader’s situation.

Give them something they can use immediately: a concrete check, choice or next step.

Try this with one article today. Write down a question a prospective client might ask, then draft a short answer using only the relevant material from that article.

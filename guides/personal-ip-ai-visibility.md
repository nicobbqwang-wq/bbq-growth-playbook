# How to check whether AI answers describe your personal brand accurately

By BBQ Growth Lab. Method version: 2026-09-14. [简体中文](personal-ip-ai-visibility.zh-CN.md)

Start with verifiable public facts, then observe answers under recorded conditions. Track mentions, identity accuracy, source support and business outcomes separately. This guide supplies a method and blank templates; it does not report a completed platform experiment.

## Prepare the evidence

Use the [personal brand fact sheet](../templates/personal-ip-fact-sheet.en.md) for names, confirmed aliases, professional focus, public accounts and evidence-backed work. Leave unsupported achievements blank.

Use the [observation CSV](../templates/geo-observations.csv) for actual answers. Without answer samples, prepare a test plan and leave the results empty.

## What to observe

| Observation | Record | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mention | Confirmed name or alias appears | Appearance in this answer, not overall awareness |
| Identity | Each factual claim matches the public evidence | Record accurate, incorrect or unknown |
| Source support | A displayed source supports a particular claim | A link alone is insufficient; absent sources remain unknown |
| Business outcome | Attributable visits, resource use or inquiries | Requires separate records; cannot be inferred from answer text |

## Run a small, repeatable review

1. Complete the fact sheet with public claims, sources, dates and limits.
2. Separate name-based questions from questions about a customer need. Report the two groups independently.
3. Record the product entry point, visible version, search mode, date, language, region and session conditions. If a version is not shown, record that rather than guessing.
4. Preserve the actual answer and displayed sources, including unsuccessful attempts. Never synthesize missing results.
5. Check each identity claim against the evidence. Correct confirmed inconsistencies on accounts you control; use the publisher's process for third-party corrections.
6. Repeat under comparable conditions and record intervening changes. Differences are observations, not automatic proof that your edit caused the change.

## Example questions — not executed

- Identity: “What does [name or confirmed alias] work on? Include public sources I can check.”
- Expertise: “What public methods or work has [name] shared about professional content distribution?”
- Customer need: “I am a [profession] seeking more professional visibility in [market/language]. What evidence should I check when choosing a consultant?”

Do not insert your name into a customer-need question and report the resulting mention as spontaneous discovery.

## Report results honestly

For each product and question group, report attempted runs, valid answers, mentions, identity errors, source support and missing data. Keep failures visible. Blank results are not zeros.

An example report structure is: “Of [n] attempts in [product/mode], [m] returned answers and [k] mentioned the person. Identity errors: […]. Source checks: […].” These are placeholders, not measurements.

An answer citation cannot establish inclusion in model training. A public page, a mention and a qualified inquiry are different events. Google's [AI features documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) concerns Google Search and does not establish how Doubao or another product works internally.

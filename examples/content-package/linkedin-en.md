BBQ Growth Lab's content map recommends keeping each reusable unit attached to its source and boundaries.

For a figure, keep the denominator. For advice, keep the conditions. For a case, keep permission. For a quotation, keep the speaker.

The map also recommends checking derived versions when key facts in the core asset change, and displaying dates on historical viewpoints.

For your next draft, compare its opening, main claim and closing action with the source card. Resolve changed facts before sharing it.

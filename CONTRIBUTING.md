# Contributing

Useful corrections and narrowly scoped additions are welcome.

For a resource suggestion, include its official URL, the reader task, expected input and output, relevant limits, and your review date. Say whether you tested it or only read its documentation. Disclose any commercial relationship.

For a guide correction, identify the claim, explain the issue and cite the primary source. Keep platform-specific rules attached to that platform and date.

For a Skill change, include a normal input, a missing-information input and an example that could encourage unsupported claims. Report the client/version and observed results. Do not label author-written examples as executed tests.

Do not include credentials, private conversations, private client materials or invented results. Submit material you have permission to share. Original contributions are provided under this repository's MIT License.
